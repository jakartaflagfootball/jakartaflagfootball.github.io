How it works

1. Sign in with Google. This is only to make sure everyone votes once. Nobody sees who you voted for.

2. Vote in all four categories: Men's Offense, Men's Defense, Women's Offense, Women's Defense. Each team's captain nominates 1–2 players after every gameday, and you can vote for any nominee in offense or defense in their division.

3. Share. Every player has their own link. Send it to the group chat and get your people to vote.

4. Votes are final and voting closes at the time shown above (WIB). Winners are announced on JFF's socials.
