Why Flag Football

BUILT FOR TEAMS THAT DON'T PLAY SPORTS

Most team building is either a lecture or a game only the sporty people enjoy. Flag football sits in between: it's physical enough to get people moving and structured enough that the quiet analyst can run the offense.

01

Nobody starts ahead

Almost no one in Indonesia has played American football. Your CEO and your intern learn the rules at the same time, which levels the room fast.

02

No contact, no injuries

Defenders pull a flag instead of tackling. Mixed teams, different fitness levels and different ages all play on the same field safely.

03

Every play is a meeting

Before each down, the team huddles, picks a play and assigns roles in 25 seconds. It's the clearest lesson in communication and trust you'll find on grass.

04

An Olympic sport

Flag football debuts at the LA 2028 Olympics. Your team gets to try the sport Indonesia's national players are training for, coached by people from that same community.

Questions

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Do our people need any experience?

None. We assume nobody has held an American football before. The coaching is built for complete beginners.

Is it safe? We have mixed ages and fitness levels.

Yes. Flag football has no tackling or blocking. We size the games and rest breaks to the group, and anyone can sit out a round.

Where do sessions happen?

Most sessions are at Gelora Bung Karno (GBK) in Senayan. We're also happy to run it at another venue anywhere in Jabodetabek.

What should participants wear?

Sports clothes and running shoes or turf shoes. No metal studs. We provide flags and balls, and we can add anything else on request.

How many people can join?

Between 20 and 100 people.

What happens if it rains?

That's up to you. We can play through light rain, move to a later time or reschedule. We'll agree on a plan with you before the day.

Is the session in Bahasa Indonesia or English?

Either, or both. Our coaches run sessions in Bahasa Indonesia and English.

How far in advance should we book?

Three weeks is enough.
