Why Flag Football

BUILT FOR TEAMS THAT DON'T PLAY SPORTS

Most team-building is either a lecture or a game only the sporty people enjoy. Flag football sits in between: it's physical enough to get people moving and structured enough that the quiet analyst can run the offence.

01

Nobody starts ahead

Almost no one in Indonesia has played American football. Your CEO and your intern learn the rules at the same time, which levels the room fast.

02

No contact, no injuries

Defenders pull a flag instead of tackling. Mixed teams, different fitness levels and different ages all play on the same field safely.

03

Every play is a meeting

Before each down, the team huddles, picks a play and assigns roles in 25 seconds. It's the clearest lesson in communication and trust you'll find on grass.

04

An Olympic sport

Flag football debuts at the LA 2028 Olympics. Your team gets to try the sport Indonesia's national players are training for, coached by people from that same community.

How It Runs

A TEAM DAY, HOUR BY HOUR

A typical 3-hour session. We adjust it to your group's size, fitness and schedule.

[0:00]
Welcome and warm-up
Flag belts on, a quick rules talk and movement drills that double as icebreakers.
[0:20]
Skill stations
Small groups rotate through throwing, catching, flag-pulling and route running with a JFF coach at each station.
[1:00]
Draw up your plays
Each team picks a name and a captain, then designs two plays on a whiteboard. This is where the planners shine.
[1:20]
Mini tournament
Short games, JFF referees, rotating matchups so everyone plays everyone.
[2:40]
Final, awards and group photo
Top two teams play the final in front of everyone. MVP, best play and spirit awards to close.
